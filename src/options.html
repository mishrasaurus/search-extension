How to Use This Extension

This extension helps you prioritize Google, Bing, and DuckDuckGo search results from your favorite websites. Here's how to use it:

  • Adding Websites: Add websites you want to prioritize in the input field above and click "Add Website" or press Enter.
  • Website Formats: You can add multiple websites. Each website can be a domain name (e.g., "example.com") or a specific page (e.g., "example.com/page").
  • Removing Websites: To remove a website, click the "Remove" button next to it in the list above.
  • Automatic Results: When you perform a Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo search, the extension will automatically open to show prioritized results from your saved websites.
  • Opening Links: Click any result link to open it in the current tab, or hold Cmd (Mac) or Ctrl (Windows/Linux) while clicking to open in a new background tab.

Note: The extension only works on Google, Bing, and DuckDuckGo search result pages. It does not affect your search results on other websites.
